01 / The Problem

Good food is wasted.
People go hungry.

Restaurants, events and households often have safe, edible surplus food. Meanwhile, many individuals and families struggle to access reliable meals. The challenge is not only surplus or scarcity—it is connecting the two in time.

“Food should fill plates, not landfills.”

Our guiding belief at ShareBowl
🥘

Surplus food

Prepared food may remain unused after meals and events.

🤲

Food insecurity

People in vulnerable communities face barriers to nutritious food.

🌱

Environmental cost

Discarded food wastes resources and creates avoidable emissions.

🔗

Coordination gap

Donors and trusted community organisations need a simple connection.
